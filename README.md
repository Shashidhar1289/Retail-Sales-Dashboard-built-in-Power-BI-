Retail Sales Performance Dashboard
Overview
_________
The Retail Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to provide insights into retail sales performance across multiple dimensions such as region, product category, brand, and time period. This dashboard helps business stakeholders and analysts make data-driven decisions by visualizing key metrics, trends, and patterns in sales data.

Features
_________
Interactive Visualizations: Dynamic charts, graphs, and slicers for exploring sales data by region, product category, brand, and time period.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Track metrics such as total sales, revenue growth, average order value, and sales trends.
Trend Analysis: Identify seasonal patterns, top-performing products, and regions with declining or growing sales.
Data Filtering & Drill-Downs: Easily filter data by multiple dimensions and drill down to granular insights.
User-Friendly Interface: Designed for both business users and data analysts for quick decision-making.

Data Sources
______________

Sample or real retail sales data (CSV/Excel/SQL database) including:
Sales (transaction details)
Products (product info, categories, brands)
Stores (region, location)
Time (date, month, year)

Technologies Used
__________________

Power BI Desktop for data modeling and visualization

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for creating calculated columns, measures, and KPIs

Optional: Power BI Service for sharing dashboards and reports online

How to Use
___________

Download the .pbix file from the repository.
Open it in Power BI Desktop.
Connect to your data source (Excel, CSV, or SQL).
Interact with visuals, slicers, and filters to explore sales insights.

Benefits
_________

Provides actionable insights for retail managers and decision-makers.
Helps identify high-performing products, regions, and sales channels.
Supports strategic planning for inventory, marketing, and sales promotions.
